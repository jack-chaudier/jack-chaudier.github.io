Preface

Three kinds of document live here. Case studies take one system apart: the problem, the decisions and what each one cost, a concrete failure and its fix, and what I'd do differently. Memos are the recommendation memos I write after evaluating a product. Essays are the ones about how I got here. All hand-set — no CMS, no build step.
