Open source · Apache-2.0 · 2026

Pylos

The forever chat. One conversation, every model, nothing forgotten silently.

Every turn is hashed into a chain and kept exactly. Whichever model is talking gets a bounded view compiled to its budget, and whatever that view leaves out is written to a ledger instead of dropped. A deterministic 1,000,000-turn bench runs with zero model calls and verifies the chain end to end.
