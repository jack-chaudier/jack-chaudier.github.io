Abstract

I build software for operations that never fit a clean spec: real-time voice AI, internal data platforms, and dashboards people make decisions from. The work I'm best at is forward-deployed — sitting with the team doing the job, turning an ambiguous workflow into a scoped system, and shipping it in front of real users early enough that their reaction is the requirements document.

Currently building Tether,1 a caregiver-controlled voice companion, and open to new-grad software engineering and forward-deployed / customer-facing engineering roles.

Keywords forward-deployed engineering · voice & multimodal AI · internal data platforms · Go · TypeScript · Python · systems

Tether

real-time voice AI · 2026

Caregiver-controlled AI voice companion for aging parents.

Browser microphone audio streams over a Go WebSocket API into Bedrock Nova Sonic and comes back as a transcript-backed summary a caregiver can act on. Built around consent and reviewable output rather than black-box monitoring.

About the author

Jack Gaffney is a software engineer in Ann Arbor, Michigan, finishing a B.S.E. in Computer Science at the University of Michigan. He grew up between rural northern Michigan and Ann Arbor, which is roughly where his taste in software comes from: it has to fit the context people actually live in.

He likes problems where the workflow is messier than the code, interfaces that make AI inspectable rather than magical, more fiction than tech books,2 and a running top four on Letterboxd.3
